# Portfolio Credibility Policy

The portfolio is an engineering record, not a fictional agency profile.

## Rules

- Every project has an explicit status.
- Client work, internal products, prototypes and archived work are never presented as the same thing.
- No invented testimonials, clients, authors, metrics or production claims.
- Skills are described by actual technologies and use cases rather than arbitrary proficiency percentages.
- Performance, business outcomes and security claims require evidence before publication.
- Experimental AI projects are labelled as experiments.
- The interactive laboratory can contain playful interfaces, but the professional portfolio must remain understandable without them.

## Project statuses

Use:
- Client delivery
- Internal product
- Active development
- Prototype / research
- Earlier project / archived

## Public-first principle

A recruiter or prospective client should understand who Mahmoud is, what he builds, what he has actually built, and how to contact him without entering a special mode or interacting with a visual gimmick.
